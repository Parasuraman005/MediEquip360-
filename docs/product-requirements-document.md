# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## MediEquip360 - B2B Medical Equipment Procurement Platform

---

## 1. Product Vision & Objectives
MediEquip360 aims to streamline medical equipment procurement for hospitals, clinics, and biomedical facilities. By combining a rich certified equipment catalog, side-by-side technical comparison, instant quote generation, and streamlined support ticketing, MediEquip360 empowers healthcare administrators to make informed procurement decisions rapidly and reliably.

---

## 2. Target Audience
- **Hospital Procurement Officers & Directors:** Seeking certified diagnostic, imaging, surgical, and monitoring equipment with transparent compliance documentation.
- **Biomedical Engineers & Technicians:** Requiring detailed technical specifications, warranty terms, and rapid support ticketing.
- **Clinic Administrators & Practice Managers:** Looking for cost-effective certified refurbished equipment and streamlined quote requests.

---

## 3. Key Features & Functional Requirements

### 1. Advanced Equipment Catalog & Filtering
- Filter by category (Imaging, Patient Monitoring, Surgical, Laboratory, Cardiology, Emergency).
- Filter by condition (New, Certified Refurbished, Demo Unit).
- Filter by price range and manufacturer.
- Mobile/Tablet adaptive collapsible filter drawer.

### 2. Side-by-Side Equipment Comparison
- Compare up to 4 devices simultaneously across key clinical and technical specifications.

### 3. Institutional Quote Procurement
- Multi-item quote cart with quantity adjustment.
- Institutional details capture (Hospital Name, Department, PO Number).
- Immediate confirmation and toast notifications.

### 4. Technical Support & Ticketing
- Single-page desktop support portal for ticket creation and status tracking.
- Category tagging (Technical Support, Calibration, Warranty Claim).

### 5. SEO & Discoverability
- Optimized meta tags, OpenGraph cards, XML sitemap, and search crawler configuration (`robots.txt`).
- UTM campaign tracking for inbound B2B marketing attribution.
